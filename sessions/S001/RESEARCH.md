## Exact Fourier/tensor re-derivation and point of loss
Work over C; define e(a,b,c)=1[a=b]+1[b=c]+1[c=a]-1. For all equal it is 2; exactly two equal 0; all pairwise distinct -1. Put T(x,y,z)=product_{i=1}^n e(x_i,y_i,z_i). For sunflower-free A, T restricted to A^3 is diagonal with nonzero diagonal 2^n; its slice rank is |A|. By character orthogonality 1[a=b] = (1/D)sum_chi chi(a)overline{chi(b)} (and cyclic analogues). An expanded coordinate factor contains at most TWO nontrivial character occurrences among x_i,y_i,z_i. Therefore each monomial in the n-fold product has at most 2n nontrivial variable-coordinate occurrences in total; at least one of its 3 variable groups has <=2n/3. Group terms along that group. A crude upper bound for its possible low-support characters is H(D,n)=sum_{k<=floor(2n/3)} binom(n,k)(D-1)^k, whence |A|<=3H(D,n).

Let h=(D-1)/2 >=1. For k<=2n/3, multiply each summand by h^(2n/3-k)>=1; then:
H <= h^(-n/3) sum_{k<=floor(2n/3)} binom(n,k)(D-1)^k h^(n-k)
<= h^(-n/3)(D-1+h)^n
=[3(D-1)^(2/3)/2^(2/3)]^n.
For any positive integer t, A^t is sunflower-free: a global 3-sunflower would induce, in every t-block, either all-equal words or a forbidden 3-sunflower of distinct block-words; all blocks all-equal gives identical global words. Thus |A|^t<=3c_D^(nt), and t->infinity removes prefactor 3.

**Two separate losses**: (i) the 2n/3 pigeonhole is sharp at the level of monomials, with coordinate pairings XY, YZ and ZX used n/3 times each, giving exactly 2n/3 nontrivial occurrences in each variable (choose nontrivial character at each paired coordinate); (ii) number of *all* low-support character patterns is used, with no proof that restrictions of the characters to A are dependent or cancel in bulk. The binomial extension is an additional constant-rate slack but does NOT change the D exponent as D grows. A fixed delta requires shaving Omega(n) supports OR producing D^(-Omega(n)) effective dimension loss, uniformly in all D,n after allowing K^n; merely computing a smaller n-dependent prefactor does not achieve it.

## Exact low-dimensional benchmarks
U(D,1)=2: any 3 distinct letters in one coordinate form a sunflower; any 2 do not. For n=2, identify each word (a,b) with edge of a simple bipartite graph on two D-vertex parts. Three edges incident to one vertex with distinct other endpoints would be a sunflower, so sunflower-free graph has max degree <=2; three pairwise disjoint edges form a sunflower, so matching number <=2. Every bipartite graph of maximum degree <=2 is 2-edge-colourable (alternating paths/even cycles), thus its edges partition into 2 matchings each of size <=2. Hence <=4 edges. All 4 words in {0,1}^2 are sunflower-free: 3 distinct binary words always exhibit a (2,1) coordinate. Therefore **U(D,2)=4** for all D>=3. These facts are routine benchmarks, not priority claims. In general {0,1}^n witnesses U(D,n)>=2^n; Cartesian products preserve sunflower-freeness.

## Route A — global coordinate types, joint Fourier slicing (attempt; negative)
Hope: rather than the old per-term assignment to x/y/z, align Fourier supports over many coordinates and exploit cancellation or an A-dependent rank deficiency that is at least D^(epsilon n). A precise SUFFICIENT but wholly UNPROVED mechanism would be: there exist universal epsilon>0, K<infinity such that for every D>=3,n>=1 and every sunflower-free A, the restricted diagonal tensor T|A^3 admits a slice decomposition with <=(K D^(2/3-epsilon))^n slices. This tautologically gives the target (and cannot count as independent progress); require a NONTAUTOLOGICAL explicit criterion guaranteeing it. No such criterion was established. Balanced XY/YZ/ZX monomials above disprove the naive claim that every Fourier monomial has a side with <=(2/3-epsilon)n nontrivial occurrences. Character orthogonality does not permit deleting those monomials termwise: any improvement must use global dependencies/cancellation on A. Testing finite tensors cannot imply uniform asymptotic exponent. **No mechanism survives this route.**

## Route B — entropy/heavy-symbol recursion (attempt, explicitly falsified)
A tempting precise lemma is that for some fixed eta>0 and ALL D>=3,n>=1, each nonempty sunflower-free A has some coordinate i and symbol a with |{x in A:x_i=a}|/|A| >= D^(-1+eta). If it held with eta>1/3, choosing this fibre (sunflower-free after removing its fixed coordinate) and iterating would give U(D,n)<=D^((1-eta)n), i.e. the target with delta=eta-1/3,K=1. **The unconditional lemma is FALSE for every eta>0 and every D>=3**, by the explicit perfect-matching coordinate construction in the next section. In particular no purely universal per-coordinate heavy-atom induction works. The constructed examples are sparse relative to D^(2n/3); this does NOT falsify a thresholded *near-extremal* version, which is unproved and would require a new, genuinely quantitative density-sensitive theorem.

### Explicit balanced-marginal falsifier for ALL D>=3
Fix D>=3, let V be a set of 2D labelled points and N=binom(2D,2). For each unordered pair p={u,v}⊂V, fix ANY perfect matching M_p of V containing p (pair remaining 2D-2 points arbitrarily). Label the D pairs in M_p by 1,...,D. For every v∈V define vector w(v)∈[D]^N by the label of the matching pair containing v in coordinate p. The 2D vectors are pairwise distinct: for u≠v choose p={u,z} with z distinct from u,v; then u and v lie in different matched pairs. For every triple of distinct u,v,z, coordinate p={u,v} has w(u)_p=w(v)_p ≠ w(z)_p: the family is sunflower-free. At each coordinate exactly TWO vectors have each of the D symbols, so every one-coordinate marginal of uniform X∈A_D={w(v):v∈V} is EXACTLY uniform with mass 1/D. Therefore max_i,a P(X_i=a)=1/D < D^(-1+eta) for any eta>0. A_D^t gives sunflower-free, perfectly balanced marginal examples of size (2D)^t, length Nt for all t>=1, so even merely insisting |A| grow exponentially in n (with D-dependent weak base) does not repair the lemma.

Smaller independent D=3 certificate (six distinct words in [3]^3 with 0/1/2 symbols): (0,0,0),(0,0,1),(1,1,0),(1,2,2),(2,1,2),(2,2,1). Each column contains each symbol exactly twice. If a sunflower triple exists, its first coordinates must be 0,1,2 (none occurs three times). All-equal second coordinates impossible (0 versus 1/2), so the latter must be all different. The only choices of group-1 and group-2 words with different second coordinates are (1,1,0)+(2,2,1) or (1,2,2)+(2,1,2). The third coordinates in the first case are (0-or-1,0,1), and in the second (0-or-1,2,2): both have exactly two equal, contradiction. This is an explicit check, not a claim of original construction.

## Route C — density of (2,1) coordinate types (attempt; falsified as stated)
For a triple (x,y,z), let b(x,y,z) be the number of coordinates at which exactly two values agree. Sunflower-freeness merely says b>=1 for all distinct triples. A hoped-for ALL-family strengthening b>=epsilon n (or E[b]>=epsilon n for independent uniform triple) fails even for exponentially large A. Set H_m={0,e_1,...,e_m}⊂{0,1}^m, m>=3. Any distinct triple from H_m has b=2 if one is 0, and b=3 for three distinct unit vectors; in particular H_m is sunflower-free. For t blocks, H_m^t⊂[D]^(mt) has cardinality (m+1)^t = ((m+1)^(1/m))^(mt), and the three repeated products e_1^t,e_2^t,e_3^t have b=3t=(3/m)mt. Given epsilon>0, take m>3/epsilon: a fixed-rate exponential-size family violates universal b>=epsilon n. Moreover for iid uniform triple, each coordinate is Bernoulli with P(1)=1/(m+1), so P((2,1)-type)=3m/(m+1)^2 < 3/m; hence E[b]/(mt)<3/m. The same obstruction persists for t arbitrarily large. A near-NS-density type-entropy statement remains unproved and is NOT supplied by this argument.

## Random-family and prior-art stress
Independent uniform D-ary words have per-coordinate 3-sunflower compatibility probability p_D = P(all equal or all distinct)=1/D^2 + (D-1)(D-2)/D^2 = 1-3/D+3/D^2. Thus the probability a random triple is a sunflower in all n coordinates is p_D^n, near 1 when D≫n; naive random large subsets are not plausibly extremisers. Product binary subcubes and one-hot blocks establish exponential lower families but fail the D^(2/3) scale. For n fixed, standard Erdős–Rado sunflower lemma applied to n-partite n-sets gives a bound <=2^n n! independent of D; it has factorial dependence on n and does NOT solve the sought uniform-base question. No construction here contradicts the published upper bound. Direct finite examples provide falsifications of over-strong lemmas, not any uniform D-exponent gain.
